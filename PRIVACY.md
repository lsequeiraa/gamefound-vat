# Privacy policy

*VAT Included for Gamefound*, last updated 2026-09-27.

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to its developer or to any third party.

## What it reads

Only on `gamefound.com`, and only to work out which VAT rate to add to the prices shown:

- **Prices on the Gamefound page you are viewing**, which it rewrites to include VAT.
- **From Gamefound's own website, using your existing Gamefound login:** your cart for the project you are viewing (including the tax rates Gamefound applies to it), the project's list of delivery countries, and, when you are logged in, your list of Gamefound pledges and the tax details of your most recent orders (up to 10).

These requests go only to `gamefound.com`, over HTTPS, exactly as the Gamefound website itself makes them.

## What it stores

In your browser's extension storage, on your device only:

- whether VAT is switched on or off;
- the VAT rate learned from your recent orders for each delivery country (for example "Portugal: 23%"), with the time it was learned, refreshed at most once a day.

Nothing else from your cart or orders is kept. Removing the extension deletes this data.

## Sharing

None. No data leaves your browser except the requests to `gamefound.com` described above.

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
